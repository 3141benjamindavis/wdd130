My Coursework

WWR

  • WWR stands for white water rafting.
  • This project was designed to walk through the website creation process.
  • It also taught how to plan out a website in advance with a site plan.

ICE

  • ICE stands for in-class exercise.
  • These are difficult challenges where we get one hour to design a website to match a reference image.
  • All of the challenges were designed to teach different concepts, like grids, forms, or icons.
